Support

All handyaddons add-ons · last updated 11 September 2026

These add-ons are built and supported by a single developer. There is no ticket system and no chatbot in between — you write, and the person who wrote the code reads it.

Get in touch

Email pobi.olex@gmail.com for bug reports, questions, feature requests and anything else. Messages in English and Ukrainian are both welcome.

Expect a reply within two working days. A fix takes as long as the bug deserves; if something blocks your work, say so in the subject line and it goes to the front of the queue.

What to include in a bug report

You do not have to write any of this — a sentence saying what went wrong is already useful. But the more of the following you include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • Which add-on, and which browser on which operating system.
  • Where you were working: the sidebar or a separate window, and which mode.
  • The content involved, if you are able to share it. A screenshot works too.
  • Your document's locale, if the problem concerns separators or number formats. It is under File, then Settings.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

Please do not send the document itself, and please strip anything confidential out of screenshots. Nothing in a support message is needed to identify you, and nothing sent this way is stored beyond the email thread.

Before you write

Some things are boundaries rather than bugs — a browser restriction, or a limit Google imposes on its own documents. Each add-on's are listed in the known limitations section of the terms of use. The most common one by far: in Firefox, an add-on window opens as a modal dialog, so it cannot be dragged and the document behind it cannot be clicked.

Uninstalling

Cell Editor. Open the Extensions menu in Google Sheets, choose Add-ons, then Manage add-ons, and remove Cell Editor.

Your preferences go with the add-on. Nothing of yours is left behind anywhere, because nothing of yours was ever held outside your own Google account.

The documents

Each add-on's privacy policy explains what that add-on does and does not do with your data. The terms of use cover the licence, the warranty position and the known limitations, and apply to every add-on.
